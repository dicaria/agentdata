# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Keep discussions professional and on-topic. Examples of unacceptable behavior:
harassment, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information, and other conduct a reasonable professional would find
inappropriate in a work context.

## Scope

This applies to the project's issues, pull requests, and any other project
spaces, and when an individual represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to **contact@dicaria.com**. Maintainers will review and
respond, and may remove comments, issues, or access for violations.
